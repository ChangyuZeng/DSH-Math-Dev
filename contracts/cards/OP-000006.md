## Problem statement

Let $D=2^{20}=1048576$. Construct an ordered sequence of 15 pairs of integers $(U_i,V_i)$, $1\leq i\leq 15$, such that

$$U_i\geq0,\qquad V_i\geq0,\qquad U_i+V_i\leq D.$$

For every choice of signs encoded by $Z_i\in\{V_i,-U_i\}$, define

$$T(Z)=\sum_{i=1}^{15}Z_i,\qquad P_k(Z)=\sum_{i=1}^{k}Z_i\quad(1\leq k\leq15).$$

The score of the ordered pairs is

$$H(U,V)=\min_{Z_i\in\{V_i,-U_i\}}\ \max_{1\leq k\leq15}\left|2P_k(Z)-T(Z)\right|.$$

The required construction must satisfy

$$H(U,V)\geq1179649,$$

which is equivalent to $H(U,V)/D\geq9/8+2^{-20}$.

## Definitions and assumptions

- All $U_i$ and $V_i$ must be JSON integers, not floating-point values or booleans.
- The ordered sequence itself is part of the construction. All 30 integers and their order may change.
- The minimum is over all $2^{15}=32768$ possible choices of $Z$, and the maximum is over every prefix, including $k=15$. The order of the minimum and maximum must not be exchanged.
- The fixed dyadic grid is a restriction of the real-valued ring-loading instance in the cited Problem 6.61. It is not a claim about optimality over all real constructions.
- No fixed core, reversal symmetry, or other structural restriction is allowed beyond the constraints stated above.

## Solution requirements

Submit a witness `pairs: [[U_1,V_1],... [U_15,V_15]]` and the reduced exact fraction $H(U,V)/D$. Independently verify the input constraints and compute $H$ by exact integer arithmetic over all 32768 choices and all 15 prefixes of each choice. A witness passes only if $H\geq1179649$; $H=1179648$ is the known $9/8$ baseline and is not a new result.

A valid witness establishes this finite construction and, by direct instantiation of the cited definition, a lower bound for the general ring-loading discrepancy constant. It does not establish optimality for the 15-pair instance or determine the general constant. Failure to find a witness does not prove nonexistence. Floating-point scores, sampled routes, fixed-core insertions, and an unchanged public construction do not satisfy the requirement. Any additional structural or novelty claim requires separate source and mathematical review.
